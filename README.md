## Projects in this Repository

### 1. [1S × 6 to 6S Series Battery Combiner](./1S_to_6S_Battery_Combiner/)
- **Description:** 4-layer power distribution PCB that connects six individual 1S batteries in series to produce a single 6S output.
- **Inputs:** 6× AMASS XT30-M vertical connectors.
- **Output:** 1× AMASS XT60-F vertical high-current socket.
- **Monitoring:** 1× JST-XH 7-Pin 2.50mm balance port for cell balancing.
- **Current Rating:** 32A continuous (dual parallel 3.5mm power buses), up to 60A burst.
- **Status:** **Production Ready (v1.0)** — Verified with KiCad DRC/ERC; Gerbers generated.
